## 2. Source and original meaning of the 12 rows

The 12 flags are stored in:

`manifests/oty2/oty2_s0m_s1l_frame_eligibility_ledger.csv`

They are generated by:

`tools/diagnostics/run_oty2_s0m_mask_anchor_pose_mapping_audit.py::select_s1l_candidates(...)`

The function first restricts input to mapping-anchor eligibility values `calibration_gold`, `calibration_usable`, `heldout_gold`, and `heldout_usable`. It then groups by vehicle, sorts by SAR GT center azimuth and radius, and selects at most four rows per vehicle: first, approximately one-third, approximately two-thirds, and last.

This is neither `head(12)` nor `[:12]`; it is not random, not a fixed temporal step, not one row from every thread, not a pilot/smoke seed, and not a continuous-segment definition. Only three GM_RM017 vehicles had mapping-eligible rows, so four representatives from each produced 12 previews.

The source ledger's `selection_status=candidate_only_s1l_not_started` meant only a future shortlist. S0-MV therefore records `selected_as_preview=true`, `selected_as_pilot=false`, and `selected_as_formal_input=false` for all 12 rows.
