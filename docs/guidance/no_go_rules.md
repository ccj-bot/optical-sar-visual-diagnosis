# No-Go Rules

These are hard prohibitions for this repository.

- Do not restore the old `current_good_proxy` as a localization reliability proxy.
- Do not turn shared-offset, posthoc IoU, or final boxes into selector rules.
- Do not use final boxes to backfill SAR-only runtime priors.
- Do not treat C1.4 frozen rank as a calibrated selector.
- Do not treat structural candidate presence as structural selector success.
- Do not directly generalize GM_RM019 conclusions to GM_RM011.
- Do not mistake an A008-equivalent partial factor table for a frozen ranked candidate table.
- Do not tune candidate generation from posthoc results.
- Do not advance threshold work without visual review.
- Do not copy raw large image data into Git.
- Do not modify the old formal pipeline.
- Do not enter G2.
- Do not add A008 scoring.
- Do not hide missing fields through synthetic defaults.
